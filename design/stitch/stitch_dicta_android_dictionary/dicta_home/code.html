luminous

ADJ.
/ˈluː.mɪ.nəs/ UK & US
1
发光的；明亮的 Producing or reflecting a bright, steady, and radiant light.
2
清澈的；令人愉悦的 Clear, lucid, and effortless to comprehend; full of brilliance.
format_quote Literary Context / 经典例句

“The evening sky turned a luminous amber above the quiet lake.”

傍晚的天空在静谧的湖泊上方泛着明澈的琥珀色光芒。

history_edu

Etymology: From Latin luminosus, from lumen (light). Refers to producing or diffusing a radiant ambient glow without harshness.
